# Making invoices — a short guide

This is everything you need to know to make and send an invoice from your
dashboard. It also explains a few things the app will *not* let you do, and why
— so there are no surprises later.

Keep this handy for the first week. After that you won't need it.

---

## The quick version

1. Open **Invoices** in your dashboard.
2. Tap **New invoice**.
3. Pick the customer.
4. Tap the services you did. Set how many.
5. Tap **Finalise invoice**.
6. Tap **Open the PDF** to check it, then **Share** to send it.

That's it — under a minute, from your phone, standing at the door.

---

## Making an invoice, step by step

**Pick the customer.** Start typing their name, company, or town. The people you
invoiced most recently are at the top. If they're new, tap **New customer**, fill
in their details once, and they're saved for next time.

**Add what you did.** Tap **Add line**, then tap a service — *Algemene
schoonmaak*, *Wissel schoonmaak*, and so on. Tap the same one again to add
another (2, 3…). Anything not in the list? Tap **Something else** and type it in.

**Giving a discount?** Tap **Add line → Discount** and enter the amount. It shows
on the invoice as a line the customer can see, and the btw sorts itself out.

**Check the total** at the bottom of the screen. It updates as you go.

**Finalise.** When it looks right, tap **Finalise invoice**. This is the moment
the invoice becomes real — it gets its number and is saved for good.

**Look before you send.** Tap **Open the PDF** and read it. Then **Share** to send
it by WhatsApp or email.

## The things worth understanding

### btw is handled for you

- **Private customers** → 9%, already included in the price you see. A €90 clean
  shows as €90 on the invoice.
- **Business customers** → 21%, added on top.

The app knows which is which from the customer, so you don't have to think about
it. The little note under a service reminds you: *9% for cleaning inside a home,
21% for outside work and businesses.* If you're ever unsure which applies, ask
your accountant — the app can't decide that for you.

### The invoice number can't be changed

Numbers run in order — `202608-0003`, then `0004`, and so on — continuing from
where Digiboox left off. **The law requires this, with no gaps and no repeats.**
So the app hands out the number itself, and you can't edit it. This is normal and
it protects you.

### A sent invoice can't be edited — this is the big one

Once you finalise an invoice, **it's locked.** You cannot change the price, the
customer, or anything else. That's not the app being awkward — a sent invoice is
a legal document, and Dutch rules say it can't be quietly altered afterwards.

Made a mistake? **Void it** (there's a button on the invoice list) and make a new
one. The wrong invoice stays on file marked "void" — that's correct and expected
— and the fresh one gets the next number.

So the rule is simple: **check the PDF before you share it.** After it's sent, a
mistake means void-and-redo, not edit.

### Practice mode — try it without worry

Tick **Practice invoice** before you add anything, and the invoice is marked as a
test. It gets a `TEST` number instead of a real one, it never affects your real
numbering, and it's stamped **TESTFACTUUR** so it can't be mistaken for a real
bill. Use it as much as you like to get comfortable.

### If you lose signal

You can build the invoice anywhere, but **finalising needs an internet
connection** — that's how the number is kept unique. If you're somewhere with no
signal (a stairwell, a basement), the app saves your work and tells you to
finalise once you're back online. Nothing is lost. Finish it from the car.

## What this does *not* replace

**Digiboox is still your bookkeeping.** This app makes and sends invoices — that's
all. It does not do your btw-aangifte, your expenses, or your bank. So after you
send an invoice here, **enter it in Digiboox too**, the same as always. It's a
minute at your desk instead of ten on someone's doorstep — but it still needs
doing, or your quarterly return won't match.

Think of it this way: **this app is for the moment a customer wants an invoice
now. Digiboox is for the books.**

## Quick answers

**Can I resend an invoice?** Yes. Find it in the list, tap **View** to check it or
**Share** to send it again.

**Someone paid — how do I record it?** Tap **Mark paid** on the invoice. It's just
for your own overview here; the real record is still Digiboox.

**Where do old invoices live?** All of them stay in the list, searchable by number
or name, with the PDF kept safe. You can open any of them any time.

**What are the two numbers at the top of the Invoices screen?**
*Invoiced this month* and *Outstanding* (money still owed to you). These count only
real invoices — practice ones are ignored.

**A customer wants it in English?** New customers can be set either way, and the
invoice comes out in that language.
